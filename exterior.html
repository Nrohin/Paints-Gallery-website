Which KNP Nerolac exterior paint is best for Nepal's monsoon season?

KNP Nerolac Excel Total and Excel Mica Marble both offer advanced waterproofing and fungal resistance, making them ideal for heavy monsoon rainfall in Biratnagar, Morang, Sunsari, and Eastern Nepal. Excel Total bridges hairline cracks up to 2mm, preventing water seepage.

How much exterior paint do I need for my house?

Use our paint budget calculator for an accurate estimate. As a general guide, 1 litre covers approximately 8–10 sq. metres per coat on textured exterior walls. Most Nepalese homes need 2 coats for lasting protection against Himalayan sun and monsoon.

What's the difference between Premium and Popular exterior paint ranges?

Premium range (Excel Total, Excel Mica Marble, Excel Anti Peel) offers 10–12 year warranty, superior UV resistance, crack bridging, and dirt resistance. Popular range (Suraksha Plus) gives reliable Colour Lock+ protection at an affordable price — best for budget-conscious homeowners.

How does Nepal's climate affect exterior paint durability?

Nepal's extreme climate — intense Himalayan UV at altitude, heavy monsoon humidity, and Terai region temperature swings (10°C to 40°C+) — demands paints with high UV resistance, fungal protection, and flexible films. KNP Nerolac Excel range is engineered for these conditions.
